My Journey as a CGI Animator & Developer

===========================================

Hello! I'm Gökhan, a CGI artist with a deep passion for 3D character animation and game development. In this article, I want to share my journey and the processes I've discovered along the way. My goal is to guide and inspire those who are looking to step into this field. If you're ready, let's embark on this journey together!


Introduction to the World of CGI


First, I'd like to talk about how I entered the world of CGI. The interest I had in computer games as a child naturally drew me toward this field. I became fascinated by the magic of animation and graphic design underlying these games. This passion drove me to pursue university education in computer engineering and graphic design. The fundamental knowledge I gained during my studies prepared me well for my career as a CG artist.

The Educational Process
The education I received during my university years played a massive role in helping me step into the world of CGI animation. I started with courses focusing on 3D modeling and animation. Thanks to these classes, I learned how to use industry-standard software like Blender and Maya. I continuously improved my animation skills alongside various modeling techniques. My very first projects involved bringing 3D characters that I designed myself to life through animation.


Practical Experience
Alongside theory, I soon realized that practice is just as vital. In the time I spent after school, I worked across various studios and independent projects. This process gave me the opportunity to learn teamwork, project management, and how to work more efficiently under tight deadlines. Collaborating with artists from different disciplines on these projects has been an invaluable experience for me.


Animation software in action at a workstation.

Storytelling with YouTube
To share what I've learned with a broader audience, I launched my YouTube channel. On my channel, I share my development processes, experiments, and behind-the-scenes looks at scenes from my projects. This platform provides an incredible space for me to express myself and engage with the community. This type of content is incredibly beneficial—not just for me, but for my followers as well. If you want to learn more about game development or animation, you can access my YouTube channel here.


Mobile and PC Game Development
Working on projects tailored for both mobile and PC is an incredibly exciting experience for me. The mobile game market is growing rapidly, offering brilliant opportunities to create content across different platforms. For this reason, I build content geared toward developing both mobile and PC games. In my projects, I place user experience at the forefront, working meticulously on gameplay mechanics alongside high-quality graphics.


The Importance of Teamwork
Teamwork holds immense importance in game and animation development pipelines. Working alongside specialists from various disciplines—especially on large-scale projects—directly impacts the success of the final product. Interdisciplinary interaction boosts creativity and introduces fresh perspectives. That's why I always strive to actively participate within teams and contribute meaningfully to projects.

A creative team brainstorming ideas for game development.

Future Projects
I have many exciting projects lined up for the future. Currently, I am in the development phase of a new mobile game. With this project, I aim to offer an engaging and highly enjoyable experience for players. The project encompasses both single-player and multiplayer modes. I plan to progress through the development stage by gathering feedback via my YouTube channel whenever the time is right.


My Inspiration
Evolving technologies and new software continuously expand what CGI artists can achieve. Discovering something new every single day keeps me deeply inspired. I constantly strive to improve myself to master new techniques and methodologies. One of the things that impacts me the most is the feedback I receive from my community. This feedback acts as both my main source of motivation and a catalyst for my professional growth.


Privacy and Security
Privacy and security are critical components of technology. Safeguarding user data has always been a top priority for me. Because of this, I take great care to develop privacy-centric solutions within my projects. Ensuring that users have full control over their own data is a responsibility I take very seriously. I continuously conduct research to expand my knowledge in this field and build secure software utilities.


Closing Words
For me, CGI animation and game development are not just a career path—they are a true passion. Thanks to my education, hands-on experiences, and interactions with my community, I continue to learn and grow every day. I wrote this to share glimpses of my journey and to spark some inspiration. If you'd like to join me on this adventure, don't forget to follow me on my social media accounts and subscribe to my YouTube channel!

Thank you, and I wish you the best of luck with your own creative projects!
